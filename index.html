Academic Excellence

Our Academic Journey

A progressive, age-appropriate curriculum designed to ignite intellectual curiosity and ensure smooth school transitions.

Ages 2.5 – 3.5 Years

Joyful Discovery & Sensory Exploration

Focusing on smooth transition from home to school through sensory play, early language sounds, social interaction, and motor coordination.

  • Phonemic Sound Play & Rhymes
  • Fine & Gross Motor Agility
  • Color & Shape Recognition
  • Social Sharing & Expressive Habits
Ages 3.5 – 5.5 Years

Foundational Literacy & Number Concepts

Structured learning integrated with experiential exploration. Developing confident reading, writing skills, mathematical logic, and scientific curiosity.

  • Guided Reading & Phonics Mastery
  • Hands-on Math & Spatial Logic
  • Nature & Environmental Wonder
  • Creative Writing & Story Craft
Classes 1 to 5 (Ages 6 – 10 Years)

Academic Rigour, Critical Thinking & Values

Our administrative and admission office operates Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM, and on Saturdays from 8:30 AM to 1:00 PM.
